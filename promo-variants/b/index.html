The rules of the register

What the measurement itself guarantees.

Only the past

Every contender sees the trading days up to its call, never one after.

Every call counts

Every call whose outcome is known is scored, right or wrong. None are filtered out.

Same test for all

Every contender is scored on exactly the same days and assets.

In the app, every forecast is stored before its outcome exists and is never rewritten. The model scored here makes exactly the app's forecasts.1

The app will not open early

This is Model history after 26 replay sessions (66 calls, January to February 2023). Here the model happens to tie always up at 59.1%, and MarketLens says “Not enough history yet (22 of 30 shared sessions)”.

A thin register proves nothing. The verdict comes from all 2,871 calls.

What it means for a beginner

A forecast is worth nothing until it has a record against the simplest guess.

Before you trust one, from an app, a newsletter or a confident friend, ask to see the envelope: when was it sealed, and did it beat “always up” on the same days?
