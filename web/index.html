Behind the lens: data & methodology

Observed prices, explicit clocks

Current mode polls free Yahoo Finance observations every five minutes, with bounded backoff on failure. Quotes may be delayed; during an open session observations older than 30 minutes are stale. Quote time and fetch time are separate. Replay uses a frozen 2022?2024 daily-close sample and a simulated clock, with no network required. SPY, IEF and GLD are exchange-traded proxies.

Probabilities and price estimates

A regularized classifier learns direction from trailing returns, volatility and instrument identity. Sigmoid calibration uses a later 40-session window, separated from at least 120 fitting sessions by purged target horizons. A separate ridge model estimates the next-session closing price. Calibration can still be unreliable; inspect Brier error, reliability counts and simple baselines in Model history.

Honest experiments

Only completed daily history enters training. Targets follow the exchange calendar. Daily retraining does not rewrite old forecasts or change holdings. Frozen model pairs are compared on shared future observations. No profit guarantee; no fees, dividends, taxes or slippage. Reset clears only the selected experiment. Legacy mode preserves the original heuristic replay.
